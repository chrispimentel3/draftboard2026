Mega Bowl 2026

Power board

Power score is expected wins divided by 14, exactly as the workbook computes it. Unplayed weeks count as zero, so the number climbs all season and only compares teams against each other within the same week.

Luck ledger

Actual wins minus expected wins. Positive means the schedule has been kind.

Paste from Yahoo

Open the league matchups page for the week, select the matchup block, copy, and paste it below. Nothing is saved until you review the parsed numbers and confirm. Pairings found in the paste are also written into the schedule.

Type scores

Edit any week directly. Leave a box empty to mark that team as not yet played.

Your data

Load the file yahoo_fetch.py writes to pull teams, pairings and scores straight from Yahoo. Importing replaces any week present in the file and leaves the rest alone. Everything otherwise lives in this browser only, so export before switching devices.

Schedule

Weeks 1 and 2 came straight from Yahoo. Fill the rest as you go, or let the paste-parser record them for you. Head-to-head records and the luck ledger only count weeks with a pairing.

xW expected wins — the average chance of beating each of the other 11 teams that week, using a logistic curve on the score gap with the week's own spread as the scale. Power season xW divided by 14. Luck real wins minus xW. All-play record against everyone, every week. CV week-to-week volatility; low means steady, high means boom or bust. Ceiling power score if every remaining week were a first-place finish.
